PROTOTYPE · THROW AWAY

Pacemark: menu bar glyph and app icon

Five candidate glyphs. Each one sits left of the session limit's utilization and must stay legible in every state the menu bar item has. Click a row, or use the bar at the bottom or the ← → keys, to highlight a candidate everywhere.

Zoom Page

1× is the real size on a Retina display. The menu bar strips are always shown both light and dark, whatever the page theme.

In context

The highlighted candidate between its neighbours: Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center, clock.

Every state

Normal · stale (percentage dimmed, values over 10 min old) · near the limit (percentage red from 90 %) · persistent error (warning sign instead of the percentage) · icon only (loading, or no values yet).

App icon

The same motif on the app icon (Finder, Dock, releases) at 128, 32 and 16 px. The colour is only a placeholder: switch it here.
